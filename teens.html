Approach

No lectures. No forced positivity.

Teens deserve to be taken seriously. Support is relatable, honest, and built around practical tools like coping menus, journaling prompts, and check-ins, not clichés.

About Sazzy
Questions

What people ask

Will you tell my parents everything?

Parent or guardian involvement is part of working with minors. Before anything starts, confidentiality and its limits are explained clearly so you know what stays private and what doesn’t.

What if I don’t know what to say?

That’s completely okay. You are not expected to have it figured out. Starting is the hardest part, and you can go at your own pace.

Can I reach out myself?

A parent or guardian needs to be involved before services begin, but teens are welcome to read this site and bring questions with them.

You don’t have to figure everything out alone.

Reach out to ask a question, learn about next steps, or inquire about a workshop. There’s no pressure and no obligation.

Send an inquiry

If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 / go to your nearest emergency department. Messages sent through this website are not monitored around the clock and are not an emergency service.
