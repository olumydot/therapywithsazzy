Privacy & disclosures.

Plain-language information about how this website works and what it does and doesn’t offer.

Professional disclosure

Osakpolor Okunkpolor (Sazzy) is a Licensed Associate Counselor (LAC) in the State of New Jersey and a Nationally Certified Counselor (NCC), practicing under clinical supervision, with a master’s degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a bachelor’s degree in Psychology. Therapy with Sazzy is an initiative of Beyond Boundaries Consulting, LLC, 377 Valley Rd #1104, Clifton, NJ 07013.

Emergencies

This website and its contact form are not monitored around the clock and are not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. For mental health crisis support in the U.S., call or text 988.

Information you share

  • The contact form creates an email message in your own email app. It is not sent through a secure clinical channel.
  • Please share only your name, contact details, and the general kind of support you want. Do not send sensitive health or clinical information through the website or ordinary email.
  • Messages are used only to respond to your inquiry and are not sold or shared for marketing.

Analytics & cookies

This website does not use advertising trackers. If basic, privacy-respecting analytics are added in the future, this page will be updated.

Minors

Services for children and adolescents require parent or guardian consent and involvement. Confidentiality and its limits are explained before services begin.

Educational materials

Articles, worksheets, and toolkits are educational self-help resources and are not a substitute for individualized professional care. Viewing the website or sending a message does not create a counseling relationship.

Accessibility

This website is designed to be readable, keyboard-navigable, and mobile friendly. If you have difficulty using any part of it, please email therapywithsazzy@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email therapywithsazzy@gmail.com or call (646) 784-5871.

If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 / go to your nearest emergency department. Messages sent through this website are not monitored around the clock and are not an emergency service.
